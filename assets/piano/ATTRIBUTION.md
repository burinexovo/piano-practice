# Piano sample attribution

Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm.
License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0).
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
Original project: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano
MP3 sample distribution: https://tonejs.github.io/audio/salamander/

30 MP3 samples are included locally and embedded as base64 in piano-practice.html for offline playback. Playback transposes nearby samples and applies a release fade. No oscillator fallback is used. These compact samples represent one dynamic layer, not the original full multilayer library.
